I help companies secure who and what can access their business data, through Microsoft Entra ID, Conditional Access, and endpoint management with Intune.<br>My focus is identity-first security: making sure only compliant, trusted devices and verified users reach company resources. That covers device onboarding across Windows, macOS, Android and iOS, compliance policies, BYOD via App Protection (MAM), and Conditional Access built on Zero Trust principles.<br>Before specializing in this field, I spent over a decade as founder and managing director of a digital agency, running client projects end to end, from first conversation to delivery and long-term support. That means I don't just configure systems. I understand the business behind them, and I can explain a security decision to a technician and a managing director in the same conversation.<br>I'm certified as Microsoft Endpoint Administrator (MD-102) and Android Enterprise Certified Expert, and currently preparing SC-300 (Identity and Access Administrator).<br>I work fully remote, in clearly scoped engagements. Open to Identity & Endpoint projects across the DACH region.
